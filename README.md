# Orderly

**Unified Order Tracking Across E-Commerce & Food Delivery Platforms**

Orderly is a cross-platform application designed to simplify how users track and manage their online orders. Instead of switching between multiple apps, emails, and notifications, Orderly consolidates updates from various e-commerce and food delivery platforms into one clean, intuitive dashboard.

The goal of Orderly is to reduce information fragmentation and create a smoother, less stressful order management experience for users with multiple active and past orders.

---

# Problem

Modern users often manage orders across:

* Amazon
* Flipkart
* Swiggy
* Zomato
* and several other platforms

Tracking orders across these services can become fragmented and frustrating:

* updates are spread across apps and emails
* important notifications get missed
* users struggle to revisit past orders quickly
* support access is often buried deep inside platforms

Orderly was designed to solve this by creating a single unified order management experience.

---

# Solution

Orderly aggregates order information, tracking updates, and notifications into one centralized dashboard.

Users can:

* view all active and completed orders in one place
* receive real-time status updates
* search and filter orders easily
* access detailed order information quickly
* redirect directly to platform support pages when needed

The experience is designed to feel lightweight, fast, and intuitive.
